Who we are

We specialise in capturing the magic of childhood

Every session is a set we build for your child — balloon garlands, backdrops, milestone boards and props chosen around the theme you want. You bring the outfits and the cake; we handle the rest.

We work by appointment only, so the studio is yours for your whole session. There's a changing room with a bed for resting the baby, a kitchen, clean washrooms and free Wi-Fi — because a shoot with a baby is never just about the camera.
