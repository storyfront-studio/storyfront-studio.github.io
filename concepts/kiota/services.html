Siblings, twins & triplets

For children over 6 months, there are three ways to do it:

  • Option 1 — no extra costBook a shoot for one child and have the other appear in a few photos.
  • Option 2 — 2nd child pays 50%One package for both babies: they share the decor setup and shoot time, each gets a name and description board where it applies, and they split the package photos.
  • Option 3 — 2nd child gets 15% offA package each, with their own decor setup and their own time slot.

Charged separately

A few extras aren't included in the package price and are added on request: toy cars, the piano, and a baby entertainer.

What you bring

Outfits are provided by the client, as is the cake for a cake smash and the milk and fruit for a milk bath shoot. Everything else — decor, props, backdrops — is ours.
