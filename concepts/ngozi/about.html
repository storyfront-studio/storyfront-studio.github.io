About

Meet Dr Imani Achieng.

Medical doctor, aesthetic practitioner, and the person who will actually look at your skin.

The founder

It started with one question.

"Why is this happening to your skin, and not just what can we put on it?"

In general practice, Dr Imani kept meeting the same patient: someone with a shelf of creams, soaps and social-media products, none of which had been chosen for their skin.

She trained further in aesthetic medicine and opened Ngozi to work the other way round. Find the trigger first. Then, and only then, build the treatment.

Ngozi means skin in Swahili. The clinic is deliberately small, so every patient sees the same doctor from the first consultation to the last follow-up.

Medical doctor Aesthetic medicine Evidence-based care
How she works

Holistic. Clinical. Personal.

Triggers, lifestyle and a proper clinical look at the skin come first. The treatment plan comes last.

  1. ConsultationIn clinic or virtually. Every treatment begins here.
  2. Triggers & lifestyleClimate, routine, diet, stress and the products you already use.
  3. Clinical evaluationSkin analysis, barrier and sensitivity checks, acne and pigmentation grading.
  4. Your planIn-clinic treatment where it helps, plus a home routine with medical-grade skincare.
