Hair tips from Tawi Botanics

Assess your hair porosity

Place a clean strand of hair into a glass of water and observe what happens:

  • If it sinks to the bottom, your hair has high porosity.
  • If it floats on the top, your hair has low porosity.
  • If it stays suspended in the middle, your hair has medium/normal porosity.

Then pick the matching Root Oil above, or message us and we'll help.

Tawi Botanics Hair Essential tips
  • Understand your hair type.
  • Avoid heavy styling products that weigh hair down.
  • Hydration is key and moisture is crucial: use rich nourishing creams and butters.
  • Deep condition regularly (at least monthly).

“Healthy hair or long hair? Well both are possible, but strive to have healthy hair first then growth is inevitable.”

How to use
  • Start product on clean hair.
  • Patch test for any allergic reaction.
  • Shake well before use.
  • Keep it in a cool and dry place.
  • For the butter, keep it refrigerated if in a hot area.
  • Not tested on kids below 4 years.
