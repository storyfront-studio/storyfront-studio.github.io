Why a website

You have Instagram. Here's what it can't do.

Google doesn't show posts

When someone searches "skin clinic Westlands", Google shows websites and map listings. Not your Instagram grid.

Fewer "how much?" DMs

Prices, hours and location in one place, so customers who are ready can act, and the rest stop quietly leaving.

It's yours

An algorithm change or a locked account can't take it away. It's your address on the internet.

How it works

Six steps, no surprises.

  1. We talk

    A short call or WhatsApp chat about your business. I send a written scope with every page and feature listed, and one firm price.

  2. Deposit

    50% to begin, or the full amount if you prefer. This reserves your slot.

  3. Discovery

    A short form about your business, your customers and the look you want, plus your photos, prices and hours. I can write the text.

  4. First version

    Starter in 2–3 business days, Standard in 5–7, counted from when the deposit and your discovery answers are both in.

  5. Changes

    Two or three rounds, depending on the package. You see everything before it goes live.

  6. Launch

    Balance paid, site live on your own domain, and it's yours.

Questions

Straight answers.

Are these real businesses?

No. Every site in this portfolio is a concept: a complete website for a fictional business, built to the same standard as paid work, so you can judge the work itself. Tap any of them and use it as a customer would.

Can we meet in person?

I work remotely, with businesses across Kenya, entirely over WhatsApp, calls and video. No travel and no office overheads is part of how the prices stay where they are. You get just as much of my attention, without the traffic.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Yes. Send me the link and I'll tell you honestly whether it needs a rebuild or just fixing.

Do I need a domain?

No. I register it in your name as part of monthly care.

Who owns the website?

You do, once the build fee is paid in full. The monthly fee is for looking after it, not a condition of owning it.

Can I pay once, with no monthly fee?

Yes. One-off builds are available if you have someone technical to look after the site. Ask me and I'll explain the trade-off honestly.

How long does it really take?

The clock starts when the deposit and your discovery answers are both in. After that: 2–3 business days for Starter, 5–7 for Standard.
