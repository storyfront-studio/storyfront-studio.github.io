Aurelle Skin Clinic

A clinic that takes bookings and M-Pesa deposits by itself, while the team is with patients.

Open the live site Want one like this?
The brief · set by Storyfront

A skin clinic with four specialists, focused on melanin-rich skin. Clients book through DMs and phone calls, and every no-show leaves a specialist with an empty hour.

The problem the design solves

The back-and-forth and the no-shows cost the clinic money. The site has to let a client choose a treatment, a specialist and a time that is genuinely free, then hold the slot with a small M-Pesa deposit, without anyone at the clinic picking up the phone.

Three decisions, and why

  1. Booking in four steps

    Treatment, specialist, a free time, then details. Every treatment and specialist card on the site jumps straight into the right step.

  2. A deposit holds the slot

    A small M-Pesa deposit by STK push confirms the booking and comes off the final bill. It's the simplest answer to no-shows, using the way clients already pay.

  3. Health details kept private

    The skin intake form is encrypted before it's stored, so only the client and the clinic can read it. A clinic's site has to earn that trust.

The Premium build: a full booking system with M-Pesa deposits

Live availability for each specialist, a slot held while the client pays, email confirmations and reminders, a client account for managing bookings, and a staff dashboard for the day's appointments.
