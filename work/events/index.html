The brief · set by Storyfront

An event planner styling weddings, proposals and baby showers across Nairobi. Couples find her through reels, then ask the same five questions in her DMs.

The problem the design solves

Every enquiry starts with a week of messages: which date, how many guests, where, what budget. The site has to show the work at full size and turn "how much?" into a complete brief before the first reply.

Three decisions, and why

  1. The work is the hero

    Large tablescape and set-up photos with filters by occasion, because in events the photos do the selling.

  2. Plan your event in four steps

    Occasion, date and guests, services and budget, then a name. It lands in her WhatsApp as one tidy, ready-to-quote brief.

  3. Luxury without the clichés

    Dark teal and champagne with a slowly turning diamond. It feels special without leaning on gold foil and script fonts.
