Tawi Hair Spa & Botanics

One shop, two businesses: treatments in the chair, products delivered countrywide.

Open the live site Want one like this?
The brief · set by Storyfront

A herbal hair spa in Lang'ata that also makes its own oils, butters and shampoos. Half the customers come in for treatments. The other half have never visited, and order on WhatsApp from across Kenya.

The problem the design solves

Two audiences and one small team. The site has to sell a spa visit and a product range without either burying the other, and every order needs to arrive already complete: product, size, price and delivery.

Three decisions, and why

  1. Two doors on the home page

    The Spa and The Shop sit side by side, each with its own mark, so visitors pick their path in one tap.

  2. A catalogue that writes the order

    Every "Order" button opens WhatsApp with the product, size, price and pick-up or delivery already filled in.

  3. Prices in the open

    The treatment menu shows real prices, which cuts down the "how much?" messages before they start.
