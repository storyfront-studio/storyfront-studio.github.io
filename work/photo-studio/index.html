The brief · set by Storyfront

A studio in Ngara that builds a themed set for every child, with balloons, backdrops and milestone boards. Parents compare studios on two things: price, and how good the sets look.

The problem the design solves

Parents ask "how much?" first. So the site leads with packages that are clear and easy to compare, then shows the sets that make the price feel easy.

Three decisions, and why

  1. Packages side by side

    Three packages with what's included and a "Most booked" tag, so parents can decide at a glance.

  2. The offer in plain words

    A monthly booking offer explained in one sentence, without the fine print hiding the catch.

  3. A booking request in four steps

    Session type, package, timing and details, sent as one tidy WhatsApp message.
