The brief · set by Storyfront

A medical doctor runs a small aesthetics clinic in Westlands. Her patients arrive tired of products that never worked, and her competition is every skincare seller on Instagram.

The problem the design solves

In skincare, a pretty website is easy. Looking trustworthy is not. The site has to read as a clinic, calm, clinical and personal, and it has to make booking a consultation the obvious next step.

Three decisions, and why

  1. Clinic, not shop

    Deep forest green, a gold accent and serif headlines. It feels considered and medical, nothing like the loud product pages it competes with.

  2. Two doors in

    Every visitor sees two clear ways to start: a virtual consultation booked straight into the calendar, or an in-clinic visit requested on WhatsApp.

  3. Method before menu

    The four-step "find the trigger first" approach comes before any treatment list. It sells the doctor's thinking, not a product.
